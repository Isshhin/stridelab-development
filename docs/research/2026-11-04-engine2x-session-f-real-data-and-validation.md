Unspecified purposes, redistribution and third-party data are excluded. Check applicable upstream terms even for an athlete-supplied file.

Record watch, firmware, chest strap vs wrist optical HR, recording mode, source device vs platform re-export, timezone and pause behavior. ## Admission procedure

Before opening participant data, a named reviewer checks source identity, version, intended endpoint, proof of authority, DUA/consent, ethics determination, permitted processors, storage, retention and withdrawal handling. Separate permissions to evaluate a frozen model, fit weights and distribute a product. Record rights for derived features and models. Unresolved means blocked. Under approved local custody only, hash the original and manifest, freeze parser version and units, preserve per-field provenance and compare records/laps/session totals to the source. Same recording exported twice is one observation. Process precise routes locally into route codes/grade summaries where possible. Keep keys, raw files, dates, identifying free text, precise maps and participant-level outputs outside Git and CI. Git may hold protocol, synthetic fixtures, reviewed nonidentifying summaries and gate status. Record a deletion custodian and test withdrawal propagation to derived tables before onboarding. No current grant is implied.

## Validation plan and predeclared decisions

| Claim | Unit, baseline and test | Failure or abstention |
|---|---|---|
| Structural software correctness | E009 source-to-parser reference, by file, lap and session. Check units, times, provenance, deduplication, truncated records, impossible values and repeat import. Compare FIT/TCX/GPX from one authorized recording when available. | Any unexplained material reference discrepancy, fabricated samples or duplicate counting blocks affected metric. Missing HR stream blocks drift even if a summary exists. |
| Physiological construct validity | E010 within-athlete stable segment after warmup. Pair same route, direction and sensor, similar conditions and RPE. Primary: typical error of speed (SD of stable paired differences divided by sqrt(2)) and eligible-run coverage. Compare unadjusted easy pace and HR at matched speed as a separate endpoint. Málaga is an independent lab agreement test; blind gas-exchange reference readers to HR estimate and report bias and limits of agreement. | No common support, fewer than three eligible runs per period, or paired change within baseline repeatability limits: insufficient for individual change. Freeze minimum useful change with intended users before unblinding. If uncertainty spans it, report inconclusive. No inference from lab agreement alone to field adaptation. |
| Predictive accuracy | Pre-session probability of completing the recorded prescribed quality dose; lock target tolerance, observation window and handling of modifications before enrollment. Compare athlete's historical completion rate, last comparable session, and a simple intent/dose model. Evaluate held-out Brier score, calibration intercept/slope and observed vs predicted risk, with athlete-cluster intervals. | No incremental predictive claim unless held-out Brier improvement over the strongest locked baseline has a 95% interval wholly above zero (baseline minus candidate). Miscalibration or inadequate events blocks probability guidance. Report abstention coverage. |
| Coaching effectiveness | After shadow validation, randomize consenting athletes to a frozen adaptive policy or a prespecified reviewed static plan with the same contact and measurement schedule. Primary outcome: change in standardized 5-km time over a prespecified block; baseline-adjusted intention-to-treat comparison. Secondary: adherence, RPE, withdrawals and reasons. Size trial for agreed worthwhile improvement, variance and attrition. | No benefit claim if interval includes zero; no practically meaningful superiority claim unless lower bound exceeds the predeclared worthwhile improvement. Per-protocol analysis is secondary. No trial or powered estimate exists. |
| Safety association | Separate event definitions for symptoms, training restriction and time loss, onset and exposure time. Start with selected-event replication in Groningen. For absolute risk obtain prospective surveillance including injury-free weeks, recurrent events and withdrawals. Compare base rate, prior injury where available, exposure alone and exposure plus recovery. Report precision-recall, calibration, false alerts per athlete-week only where denominators exist. | Block absolute-risk claims with selected controls, non-reconstructible exposure time, informative loss or too few events. No prevention claim from association. Prospectively assign an independent safety reviewer and predefine escalation/stopping rules before intervention. |

**Split and timing.** Lock an outer holdout of whole athletes for generalization and an inner chronological development/validation split. Separately evaluate personalized forecasts on a later block using only that athlete's earlier history. Keep all copies, laps and overlapping windows together; purge boundaries by full feature lookback plus outcome horizon. When dates or ordering cannot be verified, mark the forward test unavailable. Fit normalization, imputers, matching parameters and thresholds only on development data. Retain site and device holdouts when feasible. Report denominators in athletes as well as sessions. Single-athlete pilots yield descriptive within-athlete results only.

**Sensitivity and negative controls.** Use the existing candidate match windows (HR ±3 bpm, temperature ±3 °C, RH ±10 percentage points); tighten them in prespecified analyses and report lost coverage. Vary pause handling, grade source, optical vs strap HR, heat, route and prior-day exertion separately. Repeat with same-device pairs and without outliers under frozen rules. Stratify by experience, volume, sex and climate when support and confidentiality allow. Mask streams and entire weeks, simulate dropout concentrated after difficult sessions, compare complete-case and plausible best/worst-case outcomes. Preserve unknown reasons. Shuffle plan IDs within athlete and comparable intent/time blocks: persisting alignment advantage triggers leakage investigation. Shift sensor streams to break synchrony and duplicate activities: the pipeline should reject misaligned pairs and leave sample size unchanged. Never tune on these holdouts. Report baseline and candidate performance on identical eligible cases plus coverage for all planned cases.

**Prospective shadow log.** Freeze policy and baseline predictions before plan acceptance and exercise. Log available inputs, proposed change, actual choice, manual edits and outcome separately. Reviewer adjudicates ambiguous links blinded to prediction; unverified plan revisions stay unknown. Report invitation, enrollment, eligibility, missing outcomes and dropout flow. Set precision-driven enrollment before confirmatory analysis; pilot data used for design stay outside final holdout. A quiet shadow run establishes prospective prediction performance only. A controlled comparison is required for effectiveness.
