# P2-F4 | One owner, one original, one development pilot

**PACKAGE_READY_FOR_PARTICIPANT_APPROVAL** describes this blank instrument. **S1 remains QUARANTINE_PENDING_PERMISSION.** No receipt, grant, human record or real-device result is represented here. The steward must fill in the private particulars before presenting the instrument. A missing or disputed prerequisite means leave the record on the device. Ownership, a prior conversation, connector access or a previous upload does not authorize this use.

## Participant notice and private response form

The participant is the adult who wore the recording device and wrote the separate diary. The controller named below determines the pilot purpose, protects the records and handles withdrawal. Participation is voluntary. You can decline any item or stop by contacting the controller; declining a required use means the pilot cannot proceed. There is no coaching intervention or individual performance assessment.

**Purpose:** check whether one directly exported recording can be decoded and matched to the correct planned opportunity and accepted revision as they were known at the time. We will check the device measurements against an independent reference and test repeat import, replay and removal. We will use only the agreed pilot window.

| Private particulars (complete before asking for approval) | Enter privately |
|---|---|
| Scope | Notice version; one pilot window; account and study aliases; authorized opportunity and revision; allowed fields and format. |
| Origin | Adult self-recording and self-authored contemporaneous diary attestation; device class/model, firmware, sensor epoch, original direct-export steps; terms version/date, source restrictions and steward verification. No provider API, mirrored feed, converted third-party file or vendor-authored plan content. |
| Controller and review | Controller identity and contact; custodian/deletion lead; named source steward, blinded reference reviewer and comparison reviewer, access scope for each; lawful-basis and applicable ethics determination with evidence. |

| End dates | Absolute original-deletion deadline; earlier trigger on verified reconciliation; derivative close/deletion deadline; backup exclusion or precise protected backup rotation deadline; withdrawal contact, response deadline and method of confirming completion. |

**Exact data ceiling.** Select one original and just the relevant diary entry. The eligible device fields are session/lap indices, activity type and start, timestamps/offset, elapsed and timer duration, pause/resume events, sampled distance or speed with basis, HR, cadence, sensor/device epoch and sampling/gap evidence. The diary may supply a native plan/opportunity/version reference, decision and acceptance times, lock/edit state, participant choice, occurrence and optional coded reason and follow-up availability. Record separately when each item became available. Do not collect the rest of the diary. Optional declined fields stay declined; missing values are not zero.

No route coordinates are needed. Some originals contain GPS, serials or identifying timestamps. Explain those incidental fields *before* approval; if their temporary local custody is unacceptable, do not receive that original. Leave coordinates unread where possible; exclude them, serials and identifiers from normalized output, screenshots and logs. Keep only relative times and a scoped route code when needed. Exclude free text, home/work locations, contacts, unrelated activities, credentials, clinical history, vendor plans and photos. A single recording can reveal routines even without a name.

**Private choices:** record an explicit **yes / no** for each row, with notice version, timestamp and participant confirmation. Nothing is preselected; the narrower of the grant and source restrictions controls. No means no access to that operation.

No publication of individual values, onward transfer, confirmatory evaluation, cross-athlete fitting, training, production or commercial calibration. Cross-person or commercial work needs a separate purpose and authorization; **this pilot source and its derivatives remain excluded from future confirmatory evaluation**. Git may contain only disclosure-reviewed, non-identifying process results. No public participant-level metrics, rare device/attribute combinations or identifying counts. Private receipt fields: participant response and date ___; steward verification and date ___; controller acknowledgement ___; withdrawal channel given to participant ___.

## Steward decision and deletion

Mark **SOURCE_ADMITTED** privately only after checking affirmative grants for all required operations, source terms and origin, access list, storage and dates, live partition exclusion and a successful synthetic deletion/restore drill for the actual staging arrangement (including isolated second account). Record private decision, effective time, expiry and scope. If evidence is absent, expired, conflicting or incomplete, retain the pending state. Recheck at each read, link, derived view, replay and export. This blank form cannot itself authorize ingestion.

On withdrawal, expiry or purpose change, stop access immediately. Follow private lineage through originals, aliases, diary copies, normalized rows, links, segments, metrics, caches, work files, snapshots and processors. Delete, invalidate derived views and rebuild affected aggregates; record dates and processor confirmations privately. Block old snapshots and late re-import with the minimum agreed private suppression state. Keep any mandatory retained copy locked and outside processing with its basis and deadline disclosed. Backups must be excluded or isolated until agreed rotation; apply suppression **before** restored content is readable, verify absence, then confirm completion to the participant. Do not claim deletion of the participant's own original. Stop if suppression or partition exclusion cannot be maintained under the agreed retention terms. Independent reviewer clears any corrected version before restart.
