## Candidate to review

The [contract](p2-f-private-pilot-authority-custody-contract.md#9-concrete-local-first-decision-review--2026-11-07) proposes one isolated Windows workstation, an application-owned TPM 2.0 NV counter via TBS as a generation anchor, one authenticated authority ledger and broker, an independently current object/tombstone inventory, user-scope DPAPI for key wrapping and CNG authenticated encryption. An NTS time gateway is conditional for deadlines. These are candidates and obligations, not installed or verified components. The process-local [synthetic authority/custody pass](p2-f-coordinated-state-independent-review-2026-11-07.md) provides control-flow evidence only.

The review must identify the selected workstation, Windows/service identities, exact software/configuration and accountable controller, custodian and reviewer privately. No real names, serials, credentials, private paths, participant details or source records belong in repository evidence. A reviewer must disclose independence and conflicts; this brief's author cannot sign off its own proposal.

## Questions requiring an evidence-backed decision

| Topic | Review challenge | Evidence needed before approval |
|---|---|---|
| Anchor and enrollment | Can the intended service actually create/use the selected counter with an appropriate policy, and can an ordinary data restore, service reinstall, or unauthorized actor reset, replace or use its identity? | Approved target-specific design and non-destructive capability/access review; separately authorized invented-data tests of provisioning, restrictions, reset/loss, readback and failure. Prior reported TBS command enumeration alone is insufficient. |
| Sole issuance | What independently prevents two authenticated but different heads at the same counter generation, including after a rollback, service duplication or key copy? | Issuer identity and exclusivity design; durable issuance/commitment mechanism outside rollback domain; exercised duplicate, concurrency, restore and recovery cases. A matching counter and signature alone are insufficient. |
| Read/revoke fence | Where is the cross-process receipt linearization point, how is new work stopped, and how are in-flight plaintext, output and key release fenced before acknowledgement? | An explicit process/IPC and key-release sequence; identity/ACL enforcement; deterministic competing-read, receipt, revoke, crash and output tests on the actual configuration. State which already-delivered copies cannot be recalled. |
| Interrupted transitions | What is the outcome before durable intent, after an uncertain advance, after advance before head commit, and after commit before acknowledgement? | Fail-closed startup and withdrawal-channel reconciliation; verified restrictive intent; readback without blind repeat; same operation idempotence and independent target fault drills. |
| Independently current inventory | How does the service detect an old object or descendant when the authority head itself is current but the data store is restored? | Independent inventory/tombstone trust and freshness, scope and lineage binding; stale object, deleted derivative, unknown alias and missing inventory cases on actual restore path. |
| Encryption and keys | Who can create, unwrap, copy, rotate or recover keys, and how do object identity, account, scope and lineage bind to authenticated ciphertext? | Vetted implementation and configuration, nonce uniqueness, separate key custody and backup inventory, role tests and documented loss/recovery; no inference from fake-key events. |
| Trusted time | Can deadlines be enforced with authenticated freshness and bounded uncertainty without violating the offline/processor terms? | Explicit controller decision on gateway/processor, reviewed bootstrap and challenge binding, disconnect/sleep/drift/failure behavior. If unresolved, time-bound access stays closed. |
| Restore, deletion and exclusions | Can stale backups, caches and derivatives be isolated before access and independently checked without exposing the unaffected sibling account? | Actual-configuration invented A/B adverse-restore drill, inventory and scratch cleanup evidence, backup expiry and token-retention basis, documented deletion limits. |
| Privileged accounts and outputs | What happens if an administrator, compromised broker or recipient-held plaintext is outside the ordinary-user boundary? | Written threat assumptions, least-privilege/monitoring and residual-risk decision; do not claim technical recall of delivered plaintext. |

## Decision and target evidence package

The independent reviewer should produce a versioned, non-identifying decision of **accept**, **required corrections**, or **blocked**, with a finding owner, severity, evidence and retest criterion for every open item. The reviewer must explicitly decide whether the candidate can proceed to a separately scoped implementation and target qualification, or whether it needs redesign. Real-data admission requires a further decision after implementation, independent verification, actual-configuration synthetic drills and the private admission checks; design acceptance alone is insufficient.

For target qualification, prepare an invented-data-only plan that identifies the isolated test environment, exact software versions, operator roles, proposed commands and expected effects, evidence handling, teardown and stop conditions. Read-only inspection and existing reported capability evidence do not grant permission to clear a TPM, change blocked-command policy, provision NV, access participant files or credentials. Obtain any separately required authorization before state-changing hardware work.
