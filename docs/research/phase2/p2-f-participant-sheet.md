# My choice about one StrideLab recording

**Blank private approval sheet — PACKAGE_READY_FOR_PARTICIPANT_APPROVAL.** Please do not send a recording or answer here. The person responsible for the pilot must fill in the private details below and give you a copy before you decide. If anything is unclear, leave it blank and keep your file.

**Why am I being asked?** We want to see whether our software reads one recording correctly and matches it to the session you actually planned. This is a software development check. You will not receive coaching, medical advice or a performance score. Saying no, skipping optional questions or stopping later will not affect your app use.

**What would I share?** One original, directly exported, single-activity TCX file from a device you wore as an adult, for the one session identified privately below. Also, just the entry you wrote around that time: which session you planned, which version you accepted, whether you did it and when that information was recorded. Any reason for a change and any follow-up information are optional. We do not want your whole diary, past workouts, account login or someone else's plan or file.

The reviewers would check the activity and laps, times, distance, heart rate, cadence and available time-stamped samples against an independent offline reading. They would convert units, compare the two readings, flag gaps and check the plan match, duplicate handling, replay and removal. They will leave unavailable measurements unknown. **GPS is not required.** The original may nevertheless contain route coordinates, exact times or device identifiers that could reveal where you live or your routine. Those fields will not be copied into the working results. If you do not want the original held with its incidental fields, decline it.
