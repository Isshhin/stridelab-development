## R1: invariant and decision

The system needs exactly one current, durable, nonrollback, non-equivocating commitment per authority namespace. It must bind the complete canonical authority and restrictive inventory roots, enrollment identity, prior and current generations, immutable operation ID and request digest, and final disposition. It must serialize competing conditional commits and prevent stale or competing holders replacing evidence within its explicit threat model. Reset/replacement must be detected; unknown, missing or conflicting evidence closes the namespace. Recovery must never manufacture active authority from a digest, backup, local log, or incomplete state.

**TPM_WITNESS_ARCHITECTURE_ABANDONED_PENDING_REPLACEMENT.** The counter plus per-generation NV slot, write and lock construction is not selected. Its creator/deleter/writer isolation, counter-to-digest binding and behavior under noncooperating shared credentials are not established. No hierarchy, attributes, authorization, index address, slot budget, reclamation, enrollment, or retirement policy is selected; these are not implementation choices for an active design. No owner, platform or index authorization is provisioned or granted. In particular no component is authorized to create, increment, write, lock, delete, reclaim, or use the prior witness. Counter initialization, orderly jumps, overflow, unknown increment result, write/lock failures, exhaustion and clear/reset/replacement would require explicit handling in any future selection. Uncertainty closes; irreversible loss is not recovery.

SPECIFICATION_SUPPORT: the [TCG structures specification](https://trustedcomputinggroup.org/wp-content/uploads/Trusted-Platform-Module-Library-Family-2.0-Level-00-Revision-1.81-Part-2-Structures_8December2023.pdf) and [review](p2-f-custody-authority-independent-security-review-2026-11-07.md) distinguish NV locking from deletion; counter advancement is not an atomic digest commitment. WINDOWS_TBS_ACCESSIBILITY: [TBS command blocking](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/tbs/command-blocking) and [scheduling](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/tbs/command-scheduling) do not establish the desired authorization or transaction. TARGET_EVIDENCE_REQUIRED: none obtained; no unsupported-hardware conclusion follows. A replacement is not proposed by assertion: its trust roots, authorization, nonrollback conditional commit, reset and recovery must be independently specified and reviewed.

## R2: withdrawal and output

Proposed requirements, not an implemented service: a separately governed authenticated append-only withdrawal source receives authenticated participant or authorized delegate requests. Each contains namespace, scoped identity, immutable request ID, intended restriction and digest. The source assigns an immutable receipt ID and monotonically ordered sequence, durably records the request before confirming receipt, and returns signed, challenge-bound checkpoints including source epoch, namespace and high-water mark. Retention must cover recovery and reinstall; no trusted compaction without a complete authenticated snapshot and continuity proof. A source must be independently selected and its non-equivocation and availability assessed; signed checkpoints alone do not prove completeness or prevent split views.

WITHDRAWAL_RECEIVED is the broker's verified delivery of a source-durable receipt, serialized at a single output gate. NO_FURTHER_OUTPUT_ALLOWED occurs at that same gate event for the scope: no new decryption, export, publication, backup, derived data, cache population or queued work; existing workers must be fenced at every externally visible commit and canceled or drained. Previously escaped output is recorded when evidence permits; unknown ordering after crash closes. A source accepting a request is not automatically receipt at a disconnected broker: preventing output in that interval requires a fresh completeness lease/barrier or synchronous source-mediated publication, neither selected here. Thus this design is incomplete.

AUTHORITY_DURABLY_WITHDRAWN requires an independently anchored exact restrictive commitment, matching inventory and durable evidence that the publication fence drained. WITHDRAWAL_ACKNOWLEDGED means a durable, authenticated acknowledgement of that event, not mere delivery. On restart/reinstall remain closed and reconcile from a trusted cursor or authenticated full history through a fresh, source-proven complete checkpoint, then establish a continuing freshness/publication condition. Missing retention, source outage, authentication failure, invalid checkpoint, malformed entry, unknown sequence, conflicting ID/digest or unprovable completeness means CLOSED. Exact replay of an identical ID/digest is idempotent; a conflict closes. Old requests never reauthorize: apply their restriction or adjudicate closed. No restored local cursor independently proves completeness.

## R3: operation model and crash handling

Each issue, replace, withdraw, delete, restore reconciliation and reinstall reconciliation has immutable namespace-scoped operation ID, requester identity, canonical request hash, expected previous generation, exact transition, phase and final disposition. Canonical encoding must be versioned, deterministic, length-delimited, duplicate-key rejecting and domain-separated; actual schema and cryptographic algorithm suite remain to be frozen. Conditional commit must atomically bind operation ID and request hash to previous generation and resulting state. A retry with matching ID/hash returns the previously independently confirmed disposition; conflicting reuse closes. A restored local deduplication database cannot prove non-consumption. No such independent anchor has been selected, so no operation can safely advance.

Conceptual phases: CLOSED → VALIDATED → INTENT_DURABLE → ANCHOR_COMMITTED → LOCAL_RECONCILED → FINAL. Issue/replace may enter ACTIVE only with an exact anchored active state, complete live withdrawal reconciliation and all other controls. Withdraw/delete immediately fence and remain restricted until a matched restrictive anchor is confirmed; deletion also tracks pending descendants and makes no unsupported physical-erasure claim. Restore/reinstall start CLOSED, reconcile identity, anchor, inventory and channel; they never issue or reactivate by themselves.

Durability proposal: write a versioned authenticated record to a new same-volume temporary file, check all writes, flush the file using FlushFileBuffers, replace the target using the selected Windows replacement API, and establish a separately evidenced metadata/directory persistence boundary. Reopen, authenticate, validate lengths, hashes, generation and relationships at startup. Corrupt, truncated, partial, missing or unrecognized data closes. The precise atomicity and directory-metadata/power-loss contract is unresolved on the target; do not assume ReplaceFile and a file flush prove it. See [FlushFileBuffers](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/fileapi/nf-fileapi-flushfilebuffers) and [file replacement](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/fileio/moving-and-replacing-files). Journal is only a replay aid, never the nonrollback authority; its contents must match an independent anchor.

For every operation, process crash, forced termination, power loss or partial record: CLOSED_MANUAL_ADJUDICATION unless the complete exact restrictive state and independently confirmed anchor can be verified, in which case RECOVER_EXACT_RESTRICTIVE_STATE. Never reconstruct active state from partial evidence. Unknown counter outcome, absent witness, failed witness write/lock, witness without local complete state, local state without matching witness, or received withdrawal without committed local state: CLOSED_MANUAL_ADJUDICATION; if durable independent evidence cannot be recovered, PERMANENT_NAMESPACE_UNAVAILABLE. Those witness cases are historical failure analyses, not authorization to use TPM. Lost acknowledgement or identical retry: RETURN_PRIOR_CONFIRMED_RESULT only if independently established; otherwise CLOSED_MANUAL_ADJUDICATION. Different hash for an existing ID closes. These rules apply equally to all six transition types; a permanent closure is not successful recovery.

## Broker, inventory, time and encryption boundaries

A single service identity is not executable isolation. Intended service-only logon; deny interactive, remote interactive, batch and network logon except a separately justified access path. Restrict service-control changes and executable installation to trusted administrators, deny ordinary users write access. Same-token processes, stolen credentials, administrator/kernel compromise and impersonation remain outside the stated protective boundary unless an independently verified mechanism prevents them. The broker must not impersonate untrusted pipe clients to access authority. No claim of binary-level issuer isolation is made.

Require a dedicated local named pipe with explicit DACL allowing only broker server-instance creation and minimal named client access, remote clients rejected, first-instance/squatting failure closed; avoid generic-write rights that also grant instance creation. The transition gate uses a dedicated protected namespace and explicit DACL; abandonment closes, and the gate only serializes cooperating processes. Restrict authority, backup and keystore directories to their respective service/backup identities and administrators; no untrusted writes. Validate resolved paths and handles against traversal, reparse points and replacement races; unsupported protection closes. No TPM command actor is authorized under the abandoned design. Actual token, SIDs, ACLs and negative-access behavior require later qualification. [Microsoft pipe security](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/ipc/named-pipe-security-and-access-rights).

Inventory and namespace retirement remain subordinate to the independent anchor; restored inventory cannot declare itself current. Tombstone expiry never authorizes ID reuse: preserve a nonrollback retired-namespace commitment or retire the whole namespace permanently. Unknown aliases and lineage deny. Missing payload is not issuance permission; copied keys confer no authority. Changed account, OS, TPM or reinstall closes; old backups alone never become current.

Ordering generations needs no wall clock. The first read-only invented-data preflight needs no external trusted time if it makes no deadline claims and all deadline-dependent authority stays disabled. Later deadline use requires a separately trusted gateway with pinned trust roots and bootstrap independent of local wall time, authenticated signing identity, one-use broker challenge and epoch, signed interval and validity bound, and a conservative uncertainty budget covering source error, elapsed monotonic time, drift and full possible transport asymmetry. Invalidate on suspend/resume, reboot, timer-epoch change, expired attestation or gateway loss; local clock rollback cannot extend validity. Assume gateway compromise can lie; NTS authenticates exchanges but does not prove source accuracy. Precise certificate bootstrap, interval equations, drift bounds and implementation are not yet selected; deadline access remains disabled. [RFC 8915](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8915.html).

Retain v1 CNG AES-GCM and DPAPI proposal without claiming qualification. Actual implementation, nonce/key lifecycle, profile behavior, administrator/same-token assumptions, backup custody, account changes, recovery and temporary files/copies/pagefile/dumps remain unqualified; no keys or DPAPI tests are authorized.
