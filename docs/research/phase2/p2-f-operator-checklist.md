# Operator checklist for one private TCX development audit

This blank checklist is for the steward, custodian, reference reviewer, parser operator and comparison reviewer. Keep the completed copy and all evidence in the authorized private environment. Stop at the first unmet condition. If permission expires or is withdrawn during any step, stop reading and go directly to step 10.

1. **Verify the actual machine before requesting permission.** Assign named roles and least-privilege access privately. Record overlap and who can see each output. On the selected local encrypted volume verify user access, isolation from Git/cloud/CI, index/preview, offline tool behavior, temporary files, recent histories and backup boundaries. Complete the synthetic intake, withdrawal, stale restore, late reimport and unrelated-account control **on this machine**. Record each observation as `VERIFIED`, `CONFIGURED` or `UNKNOWN`. An unverified required control blocks intake. [Windows environment procedure](p2-f-windows-environment.md)

2. **Freeze the private scope.** Reserve an isolated development partition with permanent subject/alias exclusion from confirmatory evaluation. Set the procedure for binding the participant and all re-exports, corrections and derivatives before values can be read. Pin the research commit, Python, parser/schema and independent offline viewer/decoder versions. Select the reference reviewer and verify decoder independence. Fix the opportunity window, reference fields, units, scoring rules and cutoff. Set the withdrawal contact, response time, retention and backup deadlines. Copy the blank worksheets into private storage. [Audit worksheets](p2-f-private-audit-worksheets.md)

3. **Obtain the participant's private decision.** Fill in requested recording/diary scope, incidental fields, storage, people with access, purposes and deletion details before presenting the sheet. Leave every choice unselected. Ask for explicit yes/no decisions, retain the dated notice/version and give the participant a copy of their completed choices. If a required answer is no or absent, stop before transfer. Remove declined optional fields. [Participant sheet](p2-f-participant-sheet.md)

4. **Require a separate steward decision.** Check adult ownership and diary authorship, direct export and rights, current grants, scope/expiry, named roles, verified machine and drill, and active permanent exclusion. Record the steward, decision, effective time and basis privately. Continue **only** after the steward records `SOURCE_ADMITTED` for this scope. The signed participant sheet alone does not admit the source.

5. **Receive the material and register it.** Take exactly one authorized original single-activity TCX and the specified contemporaneous diary entry. Keep the original read-only. Record its private receipt/digest, first availability, source identity, scoped aliases and authorized context. Match the digest of the temporary exact working copy to the original. Recheck tool versions and grant. Isolate unexpected records, multiple activities or uncertain ownership without broadening access.

6. **Freeze the independent reference before running the harness.** The reviewer privately enumerates every opportunity, incoming observation, source session/lap and authorized comparison field from the original and independent diary/receipt evidence. Record expected owner, actual, accepted revision, time basis, units, resolution, uncertainty, unsupported fields and the reviewer's minutes. Seal the dated reference and roster read-only. Record the private seal and first exposure time. Prior harness exposure invalidates a blinded comparison.

7. **Run the pinned TCX command offline.** Supply only the authorized private inputs and approved output destination. Check source-to-actual identity, delivered plan/revision and trace membership, ordering, gaps and missing channels. Reimport identical bytes and check that one bout remains. Replay reordered events at a fixed cutoff, then compare views before and after delayed availability. Record quarantine, unsupported channels and exit state. Use synthetic controls for chronology or withdrawal scenarios absent from the record and label them separately. Keep any unexpected terminal output private. [Private command sequence](p2-f-private-command.md)

8. **Compare and adjudicate.** Verify the original seal. Match all rows, including parser-only results, missing uploads, quarantines and abstentions. Record native reference, observed reading, difference, severity, adjudicator and active minutes. Keep every original disagreement. Append a correction with prior/new versions and historical/current replay results. An uncertain edge stays quarantined. A silent wrong owner/revision, unauthorized read, duplicate dose, future-visible item or broken exclusion stops the path and all dependent use. Independent clearance is required to restart.

9. **Record the development disposition.** Report correct links over all audited reference-eligible opportunities and correct source identities over all audited incoming observations separately. Record eligible abstentions, missingness, critical discrepancies, quarantine, unsupported channels, active person-minutes and elapsed time. A zero denominator is unavailable. Distinguish agreement for this specific recording from insufficient evidence or a stopped path. Keep results private pending disclosure review.

10. **Close, verify suppression and challenge restore.** Record whether closure is scheduled, expired or participant-requested. Revoke access before reads or replay. Remove or invalidate every authorized copy and derivative, including reference, comparison, worksheet, temp/cache, backup and restore copies. Retain only agreed minimum suppression/exclusion evidence. Check denials before reopening any stale state, attempt late reimport through known aliases and confirm no historical output reappears. Challenge restore in isolated staging with synthetic controls. Use human restore material only if explicitly permitted and required by the private contract. Confirm the unrelated synthetic account still works. For every asset record the observed deletion status, method, time, residual copies and owner/deadline for recheck. Recheck protected backup expiry and notify the participant of verified results and remaining blocked copies as agreed. Unverified deletion or failed suppression stops further intake. [Deletion verification](p2-f-deletion-verification.md)

11. **Close the record.** Record final disposition, outstanding work and any restart conditions. Obtain disclosure review before sharing process findings. Keep private worksheets, logs, paths, hashes, dates, device identifiers and identifying singleton counts out of Git, issues, trackers and chat. Leave Gate 2 unsigned and P2-B inactive.
