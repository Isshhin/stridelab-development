# P2-F2 | Reconstructing a prescription and its outcome

**Draft Gate 2 linkage-schema section, version F2.1.** Protocol ownership is P2-F. Architecture is frozen at `26a8331e642c888dd73acb94755dded063baab8d`. This is a logical research contract with synthetic worked examples. It specifies what a later authorized pilot must log and reconcile. No participant records, confirmatory outcomes or new policy were used.

## Identity and evidence

Create opaque immutable IDs at the originating trusted writer, including for offline creation. Never allocate identity from a date, route, display name, summary distance, planned time or week number. Primary and foreign keys are tuples with the same account partition. An athlete key is a study pseudonym bound by the steward to an authorized account. A provider connection is a separate scoped key. Reject unknown ownership or any cross-account edge before resolving IDs. Keep the reidentification mapping, source files, coordinates, and free-text notes outside the research repository. One account's export must never reveal another account's existence.

Every event includes `account_id, athlete_id, event_id, kind, schema_version, entity_id, origin_id, recorded_at, effective_at, available_at, view_id, sequence, purpose_grant_id, split_id, lineage_class`. Include `supersedes_event_id` and `evidence_ref` when applicable. Record quality, unit and missingness with each observation. The cohort steward assigns development/pilot or sealed partition *before* acquisition. Unassigned or incompatible purpose/partition blocks access. Keep a separate, access-controlled custody log of grant versions and dates. Derivations inherit source and purpose restrictions and identify every input and algorithm version.

**State transitions.** A decision may yield KEEP, ABSTAIN_NEED_MORE_INFORMATION, NO_AUTOMATED_DECISION or HARD_HOLD according to the frozen G controls. These are separate from a choice and from what the athlete performed. Preserve blocked/abstained opportunities in coverage. Apply an accepted choice atomically only against its matching active version, valid consent, lock state and authority. A lock/unlock is itself authorized and versioned. Stale decisions remain in history with no effective change. A proposed manual override creates its own event. Dismiss/reject/expiry does not apply a prescription. Rollback appends an authorized successor and never changes the historical activity.

## Rebuild the view seen at decision time

1. Record `recorded_at` (when author/device captured the event), `effective_at` (when it applies), `performed_at` / interval for actual exercise, and `available_at` (first verifiable availability **to the deciding actor in this view**, including required review). Preserve original timezone/offset and clock precision. Record sync/ingest time separately. Store UTC instants and IANA schedule zone. For ambiguity such as a local DST fold, require offset or disambiguation before ordering. Missing/implausible clocks never guess ordering.
2. Capture the decision's trusted watermark, accessible snapshot, rights, and immutable input IDs. Include a past measurement only if observation ended, effective time arrived, and availability in that view preceded the decision; resolve ties by causal evidence and a monotonic sequence within the same view. Concurrent/unordered ties remain unavailable. A future schedule can be known in advance if already published to that view; historical features must never contain a later measurement or label.
3. Resolve the version actually published to the athlete at start via a delivery/local-view receipt. Resolve the deciding actor's earlier base version separately. If an offline client acted locally, reconstruct with its own trusted journal/watermark; server replay must exclude unsynchronized events. Unverifiable local receipts stay unknown. Select only corrections already available at cutoff. Keep latest adjudicated truth in a separate later view.
4. Fold only authorized applied revisions in parent order. Reject cycles, missing parents, stale expected versions and conflicting siblings as active history. Park out-of-order arrivals until their causal parents are present. Pending/withdrawn evidence or a retracted grant cannot validate a link; authorization is rechecked at *access time* even for historical replay.
5. Export two distinct projections: immutable decision inputs and a later observation/label view joined by verified IDs and a declared follow-up cutoff. Record event-set digest, deterministic canonical order and projection version. Separate athlete/time splits, purge overlapping feature/label windows, fit transforms on training only, and isolate pilot data permanently from the sealed set. A changed snapshot is a new version. With insufficient historical evidence, report indeterminacy instead of reconstructing an invented past.

## Actual dose, identity and source precedence

Re-import the same scoped source key + identical normalized payload/digest as one idempotent event. A changed payload for that identity needs an attested correction with previous digest and reason; quarantine silent overwrites. Two format exports need a trusted common source identifier bound to the account or independently reviewed evidence linking both to the same bout. Time/route resemblance only suggests adjudication. One canonical bout may have many sources. Choose per-field authority: prescribed dose from applicable published revision, choice/reason/RPE from the athlete's timestamped account, measured dose from authorized device observations with explicit units and scope, and endpoint from its designated independent reference. Prefer original calibrated signal over a known derivative of it for the same metric. Explicit manual occurrence can establish performance while numeric dose remains missing. If equally eligible observations materially disagree beyond the registered precision, retain both, flag conflict and exclude that field until adjudication. Never use a blanket FIT-over-TCX rule.

Completion labels are completed, partial, confirmed_skipped, substituted, unknown, with separate unplanned activity classification. Completion refers to an explicit revision and an evidence rule. Substitution preserves the original planned dose plus an explicit relation to the different performed intent. No upload implies unknown. Confirmed skip can contribute zero **performed work for that opportunity**; HR, pace and sensor measurements stay inapplicable. Missing workouts and missing measurement periods do not imply zero exposure or an overall rest day.

If one opportunity uses two bouts, sum verified non-overlapping native exposures under its one denominator (e.g. 2 km + 3 km = 5 km, one opportunity, two bouts). If one bout serves an easy run and a conditioning goal, track both intent edges. Count one bout/observed exposure in the athlete total. For dose attributed to both purposes, allocate only disjoint measured segments or record shared credit without summing. Unresolved overlap excludes attributed totals. Preserve opportunity-level and activity-level denominators separately.

| Measurement contract | Canonical units, method and validity |
|---|---|
| Time and distance | Seconds and metres. Elapsed = end minus start including pauses. Timer = accumulated running timer intervals; moving = source/algorithm-defined motion with threshold/version. Retain all three separately, event boundaries and pause/gap mask. Missing timer/moving stays missing. Do not infer active dose from elapsed time. |
| HR, pace, speed | HR bpm with chest/wrist/other sensor, device epoch, sampling and quality. Speed m/s with device speed, measured distance/time or GPS derivation and exact time basis. Pace s/km = 1000 / positive speed; stationary speed zero is valid, pace then undefined. Preserve sentinels/raw zeros and flag physiologically invalid readings rather than treating them as observed effort. |
| Power and cadence | Watts with meter/device/model, side, calibration and averaging. Running cadence steps/min total; record raw units and whether one-sided cycles, total steps or unknown. Double only if convention verified. Unknown convention blocks normalized comparison. Pedal rpm remains discipline-specific. |
| Strength, skill and conditioning | Movement/skill ID and variant, sets, reps, load kg and convention, RIR/scale, work/recovery seconds as relevant. Store discipline-native exposure and denominator; no conversion to running km. |
| Subjective and environment | RPE with instrument/range and post-exercise prompt time, fatigue/soreness as reported with scale/time. Preserve source, measurement interval, published/available time, method, uncertainty and selection rule for linked weather. A retrospective station reading cannot enter an earlier decision as observed weather. Forecasts need issue time and distinct provenance. |

**B0 segment interface.** Include source-scoped observation ID(s), canonical activity/segment IDs, account/opportunity/revision, parent sampling stream or restricted reference, segment UTC bounds and offset, timer/pause/gap, cumulative distance and reset flags, raw and normalized HR/speed/distance method and units, quality and device/sensor epoch. Store route code, direction, surface, altitude m, grade % and method, route/device bridge ID/status, temperature °C, RH %, wind m/s and direction, solar exposure and source/time, acclimation, fueling/hydration, easy intent/prior exposure, RPE 0–10 or original instrument, fatigue, recent hard/strength exposure, and withdrawal/rights. Link additional derived dew point or vapor pressure with units, inputs and algorithm version when used. Store protocol version, warm-up/exclusion bounds, eligibility flags and *all* exclusion reasons. Derived metrics are reproducible children of eligible segments; no segment means no observation. B0 owns matching windows, estimands and thresholds. Restricted raw streams remain outside Git.

## Missingness, corrections and follow-up

A numeric field has either one finite measured value (including a valid zero) or a reason-coded null: not_collected, missing_upload, unknown, invalid, conflict, not_applicable, withheld, withdrawn. For example an upload with missing HR is not_collected/unknown as supported; a confirmed skipped session has not_applicable HR. Record confirmed rest separately by scope/time. Never impute a completed dose from a target. Surfacing an unknown opportunity preserves its denominator. Derived estimates must retain eligibility counts, exclusions, source/version, reference and uncertainty under the frozen state dictionary.

Follow-up state is `not_due` before window, `awaiting` while open, `observed` only with authorized valid endpoint evidence, `overdue_missing` after window with no observation, `censored` for documented incomplete observation, or `not_applicable` by prespecified rule. Preserve late-reported observation time and availability; exclude from earlier cutoffs. Discontinuation/withdrawal has its own reason. Never label pending follow-up as no adverse event, success, failure or zero response. Log burden/declined questions separately. Baseline and candidate share opportunities and censor rules.

Append corrections with actor, causal reference, replacement payload, effective and available times and reason. Rebuild derived projections with fresh version and audit lineage. For withdrawal the steward immediately denies access, revokes grants/edges, removes affected records and derived copies from authorized stores according to F1 retention terms, triggers downstream invalidation/recomputation, and records only permitted minimal nonidentifying deletion evidence. Replay checks the exclusion filter before joining. Consent change never reveals withdrawn data to reproduce an old snapshot. Where erasure breaks reproducibility, mark that snapshot ineligible.

**Worked forward-time replay.** At 06:00 athlete A receives V1/O1/R1 (30 min easy). At 07:00 D1 observes V1 and fatigue report C1, offers KEEP (30 min) or a 20 min option. A accepts the latter at 07:03; the version check emits V2/R2 and delivery receipt at 07:04. At 08:00 A starts and stops after 12 min. At 08:16 A reports a class interruption. The trace arrives at 13:00; an independent link L1 at 14:00 verifies it against R2. D1 inputs are V1 and C1 only. The later adherence report retains original 30, delivered 20 and observed 12 min, with independent cause and source. At noon the reported interruption is known but the device duration is unavailable. Follow-up remains awaiting until its separate window and observation. All artifacts can be reproduced from these events without inventing an earlier device upload.

## F2.2 additive freeze — 2026-11-06

F2.1 remains the detailed logical source contract and `F2.1-pilot.2` remains the bounded private adapter. The [P2-F Foundation Unlock contract](p2-f-foundation-unlock.md) freezes F2.2 as an additive canonical projection contract. It clarifies, without changing the frozen Session G architecture:

- one canonical Training Exposure Ledger across planned and external training;
- separate `decision_time_view` and current-authority `current_validity_view`;
- explicit completion, plan-ownership and reason-coded numeric observation types;
- explicit source-alias adjudication and per-field conflict rather than format priority;
- derived-view lineage and invalidation after correction, revocation or withdrawal;
- a format-neutral minimum trace segment contract for later P2-B eligibility;
- replay-based migration with no invented defaults.

The synthetic reference is not a durable store, source-admission service, supported device claim or P2-B release. Status remains `P2_F_GATE_2_NOT_READY` and `P2_B_PROTOCOL_READY_DATA_NOT_READY`. Frozen Session G baseline: `26a8331e642c888dd73acb94755dded063baab8d`.
