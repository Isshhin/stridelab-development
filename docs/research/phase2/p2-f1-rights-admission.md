The default for every row is no onward transfer, publication of participant data, provider/cloud analysis, cross-athlete model fitting, confirmatory evaluation or commercial/production use. A later grant for one purpose never enables the others. In particular an analytic feature derived from a restricted source inherits its restrictions. Source flags and participant choices intersect; neither may broaden the other.

## Admission and withdrawal protocol

1. Before transfer the source steward records a private receipt with source/version, acquisition path (device original vs platform export), immutable terms reference/date, evidence of the grant, controller, named access holders, processors/jurisdictions, supported device/firmware/sensor and fields, purpose flags and time limit. Document lawful basis and applicable ethics determination. For Philippine collection consult the NPC's purpose, proportionality and retention provisions and document applicability. A secondary research exception or broad app consent is insufficient for this protocol. Independently verify a proposed source against originals. Absent proof, deny access.
2. Consent notice names the controller/contact, aim, voluntary choices, exact fields, who can read them, risks from route/time, deletion route, expiry and limits once an irreversible aggregate has lawfully been published. Record version/time separately for import, linkage, within-person derivation, cross-person analysis, fitting, frozen evaluation, aggregate publication, onward processing and commercial use. Leave unselected purposes disabled. Minors and third-party files fall outside prospective scope.
3. Write the approved retain-until date, expiry trigger, storage locations, backup rotation deadline and accountable deletion lead into the receipt **before** receiving data. A blank field blocks ingest. For direct originals propose deletion after verified reconciliation with an agreed absolute deadline; derived rows expire at pilot close or earlier withdrawal. Partner rows follow the tighter applicable term; document mandatory retention separately and restrict processing. For S4–S7 verify exact provider/DUA deadlines; no default indefinite retention. For S8/S9 no research copy, S10 documentary summaries only within their grant, S0 versioned synthetic fixtures follow repository history.
4. Use encrypted, access-logged private storage with separate key vault and participant mapping. Derive route direction/grade and weather locally, discard exact start/end coordinates as soon as authorized checks finish. Withhold location or date where unnecessary. Audit output contains counts and reasons only after disclosure review; suppress small cells and rare combinations. Raw export hashes, consent IDs traceable to people and precise timestamps belong in the restricted manifest.
5. Withdrawal, consent change, license expiry, deletion request or uncertainty immediately blocks reads and joins for the affected scope. Custodian follows lineage from originals to normalized rows, links, segments, caches, derived features, exports, processors and backups; delete or isolate any legally required retention copy, disable restore/re-import, confirm processor deletion and record completion dates. Rebuild affected aggregates where possible, invalidate analyses/splits/manifests, notify reviewer and participant under the agreed procedure. For inseparable models block use until lawful retraining or disposal. Explain prior irreversible publications at consent; never promise to recall them. If an institutional data subject contacts StrideLab, coordinate with custodian without re-identifying a participant. Restore testing must reapply withdrawal before access.
6. Rehearse with an invented account, two format copies and an independently scoped second account: withdraw first; both imports, linked diary and derivatives become unavailable, late sync stays suppressed, second account remains intact, backup restore reapplies deletion, an old split is rejected. Log pass/fail privately with reviewer and timestamp. This rehearsal is specified, not claimed executed. Any failed path blocks human acquisition.
