## Recovery decisions by failure point

| Failure | Required state after restart / recovery |
|---|---|
| Power loss or process crash before receipt/intent is durable | CLOSED. Receipt cannot be assumed to survive. Reconcile the independently authenticated request channel; ambiguity stays closed. |
| Partial intent or write, before confirmed counter advance | CLOSED; authenticate the intent and read current anchor. Resolve without guessing whether an operation took effect; missing/forged intent blocks. |
| Advance request returns unknown / crash after advance, before matching head | CLOSED; read actual enrolled anchor, never blindly re-advance. Complete only a verified restrictive intent according to the approved protocol; otherwise remain closed. |
| Head committed, audit or acknowledgement incomplete | CLOSED on restart; verify same operation, anchor, head, inventory and audit, reconcile channel, then acknowledge at most once only after fencing. No invented active authority. |
| Interrupted withdrawal/deletion | Stop processing, deny key release/output and preserve restrictive state. Track each object/descendant/copy as suppressed, pending or unverified; no deletion-complete claim until independent verification and backup obligations are satisfied. |
| Partial restore or reinstall | Inert closed scratch; no index, viewer, decrypt, sync or ordinary read. Authenticate enrolled current authority and inventory independently, reconcile channel, verify role/key boundary. Missing metadata, key, anchor, identity or unresolved copy keeps closed. |

## Invented-data-only target qualification plan

**Not yet cleared for execution:** the integrated plan requires the corrections above, an exact isolated target, independent review and any separately required authorization. It is a prospective protocol, not evidence or permission to provision a production/private-pilot TPM. Freeze versioned software/build and configuration, invented accounts A/B, roles, no-network policy, test/scratch locations, before/after state, expected result, independent observer, safe teardown and per-test stop conditions before execution. No actual participants, provider records or real keys.

1. **Baseline and normal issuance:** in an approved disposable synthetic environment, establish identity and empty/closed state; issue one A grant, verify unique generation/head/issuer binding, current inventory, scoped read and audit. A failed or no-op issuance must never create an alternative grant. Capture ordinary-user denial of raw key/data access.
2. **Replacement/currentness and competing issuance:** make a valid later state current; present older heads, mismatched anchor identities and two simultaneous/different same-generation issuance requests, cloned/restored issuer state and invalid signatures/commitments. Only one verified state may be current; unavailable/ambiguous authority denies. Repeat with injected uncertainty, using barriers rather than arbitrary sleeps.
3. **Withdrawal and deletion:** race A's read against receipt using deterministic barriers; assert the agreed receipt-to-publication order and that acknowledgement waits for fencing. Preserve previously delivered invented outputs as escaped. Restrict A, invalidate source/aliases/descendants, exercise late import; mark deletion verified only after independent checks. Verify B is unchanged.
4. **Restart and crash:** inject a stoppage before intent, after durable intent, on uncertain anchor advance, after advance/readback, after head/inventory commit, during audit and before acknowledgement. Restart CLOSED, reconcile the request channel and use the recovery table; explicitly test partial write, partial withdrawal and interrupted deletion. No blind retry or active-head manufacture.
5. **Restore, reinstall and stale backup:** create an invented A/B pre-withdrawal snapshot; withdraw A; restore into closed isolated scratch. Verify stale head, inventory, object and derivatives deny before key release, unknown aliases quarantine and B stays intact. Repeat partial restore, missing authority with key, missing key with authority, missing anchor, new/reinstalled app identity and loss/reset scenarios. Dispose scratch and verify inventory; no claim of physical media erasure.
6. **Keys and time:** test wrong, lost, unavailable, rolled-back and duplicated key material and ciphertext/AAD tampering without real keys. For any time-bound policy test approved trusted-time bootstrap, stale/replayed, uncertainty, rollback, disconnect and restart; all uncertain cases close. Unresolved key/time design is a stop, not a passing skipped case.
7. **Evidence and teardown:** each case records synthetic fixture label, exact version/configuration, expected/observed state transition, output/exposure and key-release result, deterministic fault point, reviewer, restricted evidence reference and cleanup result. Independently review completeness and disclose only non-identifying process dispositions. Any missing or unrepeatable evidence yields NOT QUALIFIED.

## Immediate stop conditions

Stop progression and keep affected scopes closed for competing authority or duplicate issuance; stale authority resurrection; a receipt lost or withdrawal not durably fenced; output/key release after the receipt boundary; ambiguous anchor, issuer, head, time or inventory; a restored withdrawn object or descendant becoming accessible; missing or unverified deletion/copy disposition; key/role/custody isolation failure; unrecoverable interrupted transition; unapproved target, command or processor; or evidence insufficient to establish an invariant. Escalate and separately review the corrected design; never infer success from an unrun scenario. Existing plaintext journal and test authority must not receive real data.
