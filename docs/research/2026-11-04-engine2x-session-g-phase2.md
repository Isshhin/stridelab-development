## Common analysis contract

Freeze eligibility, primary endpoint, direction, smallest worthwhile effect δ in endpoint units, baseline, subgroup plan, allowable false escalation/restriction, minimum useful coverage and maximum athlete burden before opening held-out outcomes. Set margins from pilot variability, athlete preference and blinded reviewers: for example, identify the smallest change in usual steady speed that would actually alter interpretation, then compare it with repeatability error. Do not invent universal numerical cutoffs before these inputs exist. No preregistered margins means no promotion decision.

Use forward-time evaluation within athlete and a separate athlete-disjoint test. Fit baselines, imputation, calibration, matching choices and thresholds on development data alone. Purge overlapping exposure/outcome windows at boundaries. Cluster intervals by athlete; report athlete counts, eligible opportunities, offers, abstentions, decisions, actuals and observed follow-ups. Distinguish unavailable, explicitly declined and out-of-domain. Report primary results on all eligible opportunities and conditional results on acted-on cases. Show risk–coverage curves and reasons by device, climate, experience, discipline and missingness. Small unsupported strata remain outside the proposed domain.

For superiority require an interval consistent with improvement beyond the agreed practical margin. For a simpler or less burdensome policy use prespecified noninferiority on primary benefit plus improved burden. For interchangeability use two-sided equivalence bounds. Report calibration slope/intercept, absolute error or Brier score as appropriate, interval coverage, and uncertainty. Compare false restrictions, escalations, unnecessary changes, missed interventions, rejection, override and prompts per athlete-week. Assess sensitivity to eligibility/window choices and missing-not-at-random follow-up. More abstention alone cannot pass. Stop on rights failure, data leakage or any violated hard restriction, lock, version or consent invariant.

## Five separate evidence gates

1. **Software correctness.** Verify deterministic replay, idempotency, account isolation, explicit acceptance, stale rejection, persistence and rollback. Retain Engine 1.5 fallback and require a later regression comparison through its real integration path.
2. **Physiological construct validity.** Establish protocol repeatability, independent criterion agreement where claimed, uncertainty and coverage. Match the reference to the claimed construct.
3. **Prediction.** Freeze the policy, beat the simple baseline by practical margins on future dates and unseen athletes, and audit calibration, missingness, climate/device and selection bias.
4. **Effectiveness.** After successful review-only shadow evaluation, prespecify a controlled comparison of reviewed recommendations vs usual planning, intention-to-treat outcomes, independent assessors, attrition and stop criteria. An unchosen alternative has no observed counterfactual outcome.
5. **Safety association.** A distinct surveillance design needs complete at-risk exposure and follow-up, independent outcome definition and absolute event denominators. An observed association cannot establish prevention. Structural violations remain immediate stops throughout.

If a study is too small to distinguish useful effects from its uncertainty, record it as inconclusive. An owner-only feasibility result can open collection, never general deployment. No track has passed its real-athlete gate in Session G.
