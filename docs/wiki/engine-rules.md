# Engine 1.5 Rules & Coaching Guardrails

Back to [[index]] · Related: [[architecture]] · [[screens]]

## Non-Negotiable Invariants
- **Weekly Mileage Budget:** `sum(week.distanceKm) == week.proposedKm` within `1e-6` (enforced via clamped water-filling + tenths reconciliation in `Engine15DailyLoadShaper` and audited by `Engine15M6IndependentPeriodizationReview`).
- **Immutable Baseline & Determinism:** `PlannedSession.difficultyBaseline` stores the unadjusted session; `validateSaved(plan)` deterministically recomputes expected output purely from `plan.request` (no `activities` argument) and verifies `Engine15WorkoutDifficultyAdjuster.matchesAdjusted`.
- **Pure Previews:** Weekly reviews and coaching suggestions never mutate plans until explicit atomic user approval (`Engine15WeeklyReview.accept`).

## Baseline Provenance & Zero-Mileage Beginners
- `Engine15IndependentPlanGenerator.previewAutoReconciled` never fabricates synthetic `runDays * 4.0` km or promotes `CONFIRMED_ZERO`, `UNKNOWN`, or `PEAK_WEEK_ONLY` mileage to `SELF_REPORTED_AVERAGE` (positive-km `LEGACY_UNSPECIFIED` retains backward compatibility).
- `CONFIRMED_ZERO` beginners—including those with positive `targetWeeklyKm`, `qualityCount > 0`, or combined strength/calisthenics—route to `Engine15TimeOnlyRunWalkBuilder` (`RunningPrescription` v4 `"TIME_ONLY_NO_DISTANCE_TARGET"`, `distanceKm = null`).

## Volume, Long-Run Caps & Race Tapers
- **`Engine15WeeklyVolumeSkeleton.multiFactorCeiling` (used by `Engine15IndependentPlanGenerator`):** Replaces the old `1.10x`/`1.15x` ceiling with compound loading progression grounded in recent sustainable mileage, `runDays`, `experience` (`3.5%–8.0%`/loading week ramp), race peak caps (`Marathon` / `Half` / `10K` / `5K`), 30-day single-session exposure bounds (`longestKm`, `SINGLE_SESSION_30D_EXPOSURE_CAP`), and 4th-week `0.80x` recovery cutbacks (`isSuppressedPreTaperRecovery` prevents double-deload before tapers). Legacy `ceiling(...)` remains untouched for shadow parity.
- **`Engine15IndependentVolumePlanner`:**
  - Returning runners start at `0.75 * longest` (`5%` progression cap when non-race) and `0.60x..0.72x` volume (`returnCeilingKm`).
  - Race goals use dual long-run caps: Week 1 `minOf(longest + 1.0, longest * 1.10)`, then `minOf(effectiveLongMaxKm, prev + stepKm, prev * 1.10)` (Marathon `34 km`, HM `22 km`, 10K `18 km`, 5K `16 km`).
  - Tapers: Marathon (12+w) `0.85/0.70/0.55`, Half/10K (10+w) `0.80/0.60`, 5K (6+w) `0.85` (`0.78` if landing on recovery week).
- **`Engine15M5WorkoutComposer`:** Taper long-run share drops to `0.28` (`0.34–0.38` Marathon/HM loading, `0.30` other, hard cap `0.40`, plus cross-week `<= 1.10 * peakLongUnits` cap so post-recovery weeks never spike `>10%`); 2-day race plans allow `minOf(longMaxUnits, tenths(weekExposure * 0.85))`. `PlanRequest.longRunMinutes` supports separate weekend long-run duration caps under `timeBudgetEnabled`.
- **Taper Quality & Neuromuscular Retention:** `Engine15QualityFamilySelection` + `Engine15QualityProgressionPolicy` cap taper quality to `<= 1` session/week with `RACE_TAPER_REDUCTION` sharpening, while `enrichNonQualitySessions` retains crisp `4 × 20s` strides (`75s` walk/jog recovery) on eligible taper weeks without adding long-run or quality fatigue.
- **Proportional Macrocycle Periodization (`Engine15MacrocyclePeriodization`):**
  - Short active builds (`4–6w`) skip `BASE` and prioritize `BUILD` (+ `PEAK` touch) + `TAPER`; medium builds (`7–11w`) balance `BASE` (`~28%`), `BUILD`, `PEAK`, and `TAPER`; long builds (`12–16+w`) allocate structured `BASE`, `BUILD`, `PEAK`, and proportional `TAPER` (`3w` Marathon `12+w`, `2w` `8–11w` / `HM/10K 10+w`, `1w` `5K 4+w` & compressed builds).
  - Beginners (`experience == 0`) & `returning == true` receive extended `BASE` (`50–65%` of loading weeks) with `0` `PEAK` weeks; "Train Your Way" (`Aerobic foundation`, `General fitness`) uses `3:1` loading-to-recovery cycles with zero taper.
  - **Phase Alignment & Advisories:** `BASE` selects durability work (`Fartlek`, `Hills`, `Continuous Tempo`, `Steady Aerobic Long Run`); `BUILD` adds lactate clearance & progressive/race-pace long runs; `PEAK` selects race-specific sharpening; deterministic `GOAL_HORIZON_SHORT`, `GOAL_READINESS_ADVISORY`, and `VOLUME_JUMP_GUARDRAIL` explanations warn on compressed/under-baselined race builds.

## Quality Workouts, Dosing Ceilings, 14-Archetype Library & Reconciliation Ladder
- **Families, Dosing & Energy-System Recovery (`Engine15QualityProgressionPolicy`, `Engine15QualityFamilySelection`):**
  - **Starting dose for new plans:** Base the fallback dose on typical recent volume rather than the future mileage target. Use the 56 days before generation. Prefer at least two distinct same-family workout observations, up to the latest four. Count a FIT/Garmin run once and cap overall-pace estimates at the no-segment-evidence ceiling. Intermediate and returning runners use a lower entry anchor when there is room above the introductory rung. These estimates are provisional. Saved plans replay the existing stored anchor and distance claims.
  - **Weekly Hard-Work Ceilings:** `VO2_INTERVAL` `6–8%` weekly km (`12–24 min`; capped at `18 min` when 2 quality/week), `THRESHOLD` `8–10%` (`16–36 min`), `TEMPO`/`STEADY`/`FARTLEK` `10–12%` (`20–45 min`), `HILLS` `8–12 min` (`<= 10 min` when 2 quality/week).
  - **Energy-System Recovery Ratios (no flat `2 min` default):** `STRIDE` `1:3–1:4` (`20s` → `60–75s` walk/jog), `HILL` `1:2–1:3` (`45–60s` → `90–120s`, `90–120s` → `2:00–2:30`), `VO2_INTERVAL` `1:0.5–1:1` (`400m` → `200m`/`70s`, `800m` → `400m`/`2:00`, `1km`/`4m` → `400m`/`2:30`), `THRESHOLD` `1:0.2–1:0.3` (`1km`/`4–5m` → `60s`, `2km`/`7–8m` → `90s`), `BROKEN_TEMPO` `1:0.15–1:0.2` (`10–15m` → `90–120s`).
  - **Beginner & Returning Rules:** `experience == 0` and `returning == true` cap at `1` quality/week, hold entry rung dose for 2 loading weeks before stepping up, and avoid track VO2max reps in favor of Fartlek, Short Hills, Continuous Tempo, and Strides (unless explicitly overridden by `allowedQualityTypes`).
- **14-Archetype Library (`Engine15WorkoutArchetype`, `RunningPrescription.archetypeId`):**
  - *Aerobic & Neuromuscular:* `Recovery run` (`Easy max + 15–30 s/km`), `Easy Aerobic Run`, `Easy Run + Strides` (`4–8 × 20s` `STRIDE`), `Aerobic Fartlek` (`4–7 × 2–3m`).
  - *Hills:* `Short Hill Sprints` (`6–8 × 45–60s`), `Long Aerobic Hills` (`4–6 × 90s–2m`) — explicit uphill effort/grade cues and uphill-adjusted pace bounds slower than flat interval pace.
  - *Threshold & Tempo:* `Continuous Tempo` (`12–30m`), `Broken Tempo` (`2×10m`–`3×15m`), `Threshold Cruise Intervals` (both distance-based `1km`/`2km` and time-based `4–8m`).
  - *VO2max & Speed Endurance:* `Short Track Repeats` (`6–12 × 400m`), `Classic VO2max Intervals` (distance-based `800m`/`1km` and time-based `3–4m`), `Speed-Endurance Pyramid` (`400-800-1200-800-400m` / `1km-2km-1km-800m`, mixed `VO2_INTERVAL` + `THRESHOLD` intents).
  - *Long Runs:* `Steady Aerobic Long Run`, `Fast-Finish / Race-Pace Long Run` (75/25 `STEADY`, `4–10 km` HM `RACE_PACE`, or `6–14 km` Marathon `RACE_PACE` finish on Build/Peak loading weeks only; never on recovery, taper, or returning-runner plans).
  - *Goal & Train Your Way Selection:* `5K`, `10K`, `Half marathon`, `Marathon`, plus `Train Your Way` (`Aerobic foundation` → Fartlek/Hills/Strides/Steady Long; `General fitness` → Threshold/Broken Tempo/Fartlek/VO2max/Fast-Finish Long).
  - *Phase and slot selection:* Select available, spaced quality slots before assigning a family. Explicit family preferences and allowed families constrain the pool. Automatic selection adds race-specific work in Build/Peak, then reduces to one sharpening session in the first taper week and keeps the final 10K taper week free of full quality sessions. Dose still has to fit work, recovery and bookends in the selected slot. A 10K Base rotation can pair short and long hills in one week.
- **Persisted Targets (`RunningStepV2` & `RunningSegment` in `EngineContracts.kt`):** Persists `stepIntent` (`RunningStepIntent`), `targetPaceMinSecPerKm`, `targetPaceMaxSecPerKm` (`Engine15PaceZoneEstimator.paceBoundsForIntent`), and strictly optional `targetHrMinBpm` / `targetHrMaxBpm` (populated only from verified 5-zone athlete HR profiles via `hrBoundsForIntent`, never invented `220 - age`). Distance-based `RunningStepV2` steps keep `meters != null && seconds == null` while timed WU/CD/recoveries reconcile exact session `meters`. Pre-Step-01/02 snapshots decode safely (`null` defaults) and pass `validateSaved` via `RunningPrescription.matchesStructuralBaseline`.
- **4-Step Quality Reconciliation Ladder (`previewAutoReconciled`):**
  1. Reconcile rounding and flexible weekly quality-slot sizing (`Engine15DailyLoadShaper` `minQualitySlotKm`, respecting `timeBudgetEnabled`) + release conflicting `qualityDays` if needed.
  2. Adjust warm-up and cool-down strictly within safe physiological floors (**`>= 8 min` warm-up / `>= 5 min` cool-down**, combined `13..90 min`, verified by `Engine15M5TimedDistanceReconciler.bookendSafetyWarning()`), falling back from distance-based to time-based rungs when slot size requires.
  3. Step down to the highest safe lower-dose rung in the same quality family ladder when current dose cannot fit safely.
  4. Return explicit `Deferred("QUALITY_RECONCILIATION_UNSAFE_OR_INFEASIBLE", ...)` for review rather than silently erasing `qualityCount = 0`.
- **3-Tier Intensity Zones (`Engine15PaceZoneEstimator`):** (1) Explicit race/TT benchmark → (2) 56-day historical run blend (Riegel exponent `1.06`) → (3) `request.planningPace`.

## Daily Load Shaping & Multi-Discipline Scheduling
- **`Engine15DailyLoadShaper`:** Shapes non-long runs with `QUALITY 1.15x`, `AEROBIC_EASY 1.10x`, `STANDARD_EASY 1.00x`, `RECOVERY_POST_HARD 0.76x/0.82x`, `RECOVERY_PRE_LONG 0.76x/0.85x`, `RECOVERY_SANDWICHED 0.68x` (including Sunday Long → Monday Recovery).
- **`Engine15StaticMultiDisciplinePlanner`:** Splits `place` → `reshapeCombinedRuns` → `annotateCombinedNotes`; enforces Tier-3 `sameDay.size < 2` and `dailyMinutes` (when `timeBudgetEnabled`), ranks same-day pairs (`empty < easy+upper < easy+hard < hard+hard`), and passes `externalHardDates` + `maxMinutesBySessionId` into `Engine15DailyLoadShaper` and `validateSaved`.
- **Non-Running Prescriptions:** The named Strength, Calisthenics and Conditioning goals use the shared 3:1 macrocycle. Strength uses sets, reps, hold seconds and capped ground contacts. Calisthenics uses capacity-gated reps and holds, including floor-supported inversion. Conditioning uses timed work and recovery for aerobic, EMOM, AMRAP and threshold goals. Saved plans with historical goals keep their original composition and validation contracts.
- **Spacing and recovery:** Inside a managed plan, structured hard lower-body, plyometric and interval sessions leave at least one full day between hard exposures, including across week boundaries. Introductory Plyometrics starts at 12 ground contacts without a strength prerequisite. A returner holds that dose through loading weeks. Recovery weeks and approved recovery adjustments omit jumps.
- **Independent tracks:** Isometrics and Plyometrics have separate builder selections and plans, while their existing `str` request field and goal preserve the saved schema. Separate Engine 1.5 tracks can activate on shared dates. Flexible non-running plans prefer space around other workouts, and the preview flags hard efforts that fall close together across plans. Same-track and legacy conflict checks remain in place. A deliberately combined plan still enforces its own recovery, fixed slots and daily budget.
- **Running setup:** A new plan starts from its own settings. Typical weekly volume uses up to three completed weeks with recorded distance, with at least two usable weeks required. It remains a partial estimate. Longest recent run uses the last 30 days of saved run distances. A manual override or an explicit no-recent-running confirmation retains its own provenance.
- **Non-running review:** Weekly review and feedback can recommend rest from explicit logged evidence. Step 06 can preview a reduced-dose week after recorded missed sessions or repeated high effort. It retains dates and duration or reduces it, reports sets/reps, hold or work seconds, and contacts in their own units, validates the entire candidate and supports rollback. No make-up work is added.

## Difficulty Scaling (`Engine15WorkoutDifficultyAdjuster`)
- Reversible steps `-2` (*Much easier*) .. `0` (*Coached*) .. `+2` (*Much harder*), always recomputed from `session.baselineSession()` (zero cumulative rounding drift, exact `Reset` on `0`, zero catch-up mileage).
- Quality `-2` converts to `"Recovery run (eased)"`; `-1/+1/+2` scales interval reps/duration/distance; resistance workouts scale sets/reps + append RIR cues (`ExerciseAlternatives` provides easier/harder swaps).

## Adaptation, Linking, Matching & Post-Run Execution Analysis
- **Preview-Only Local Adaptation:** `Engine15Feedback` (next 1–4 days, single session) and `Engine15WeeklyReview` (next 7 days) attach `SessionSuggestion` overlays after explicit approval. Running may use a time-only alternative, while non-running targets use rest or the separate Step 06 dose review.
- **Weekly Review Guardrails:** Blocks `Direction.PROGRESS` on scheduled recovery (`weekIndex % 4 == 3`, excluding suppressed pre-taper peak) and race taper weeks; clamps `long`-run progression within `<= 10%` of recent longest distance; requires explicit `effort in 1..5` RPE corroboration.
- **Confidence-Scored `.FIT` Auto-Matcher (`Engine15ActivityAutoMatcher`):**
  - Evaluates running activities (`DashboardSummary.isRun`) against open running sessions within `[-1, +1]` calendar days using weighted scores (`0.45` date + `0.40` distance/duration volume + `0.15` soft plausibility — slow runs on quality days are never disqualified).
  - `>= 0.80` same-day + unambiguous margin (`>= 0.15`) → `AUTO_LINKED`; `0.50..0.79`, off-day shifts, multi-run same-day contention, or `< 0.15` ties → `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`; `< 0.50` → `UNMATCHED`.
  - **Manual Override Supremacy & Adherence Separation:** Never overwrites `MANUALLY_LINKED` / `MANUALLY_REJECTED` (manual linking remains `ActivityPlanLink`, `recordedDate - 3d .. recordedDate`); matching never marks workout quality adherence as passed.
  - Persists locally in `TrainingStore` (`kind = "workout"`, prefixes `"activity-match-"` & `"execution-analysis-"`, never queued to cloud `outbox`), reconciled on FIT/Garmin import and manual link/reject actions.
- **Post-Run Execution Analyzer (`Engine15ExecutionAnalyzer`):**
  - **Evidence Tiers:** `LAP_AND_RECORD` (structured/step laps + sample stream) → `RECORD_WINDOW` (best-fit non-overlapping sliding/tail windows over contiguous FIT samples) → `SUMMARY_ONLY`.
  - **Intent-Specific Verdicts (`ExecutionVerdict`):** `AEROBIC_EASY`/`RECOVERY` too fast → `INTENSITY_MISMATCH_TOO_FAST`; `TEMPO`/`THRESHOLD`/`VO2_INTERVAL`/`RACE_PACE` (including `TRANSITION` fast-finish Long Runs) easy jog over planned work → `VOLUME_ONLY_MATCH` (`qualityStimulusVerified = false`); `HILL`/`STRIDE` verify elevation gain (`>= 15–20 m`) or short effort surges while tolerating steep uphill slowdowns and short-sprint GPS lag; cut-short runs → `PARTIALLY_EXECUTED`.
  - **Optional HR Metrics (`ExecutionHrZoneProfile`):** Computes `hrZoneCompliancePct` and aerobic `Pa:Hr` decoupling strictly from verified Garmin or FIT 5-zone settings (null otherwise, never `220 - age`).
- **Running debrief:** Pulse and zone observations require a verified five-zone profile and covered telemetry. Time-weighted comparisons exclude pauses, gaps over 30 seconds and invalid values. Predominant Zones 1–2 support a positive Easy or Long Run finding. Later split comparisons omit isolated partial or pause-affected laps when comparable coverage remains.
- **Steady-run evidence:** Compare pace halves after 20 minutes and stable HR or running dynamics after 45 minutes. Pulse and dynamics comparisons require comparable pace. Ground contact can show stability or a late increase when measured across both halves. The three ranked highlights keep the main takeaways scannable. Additional supported observations remain available as prose. Optional weather context is reserved for future use.
- **Training Effect:** Aerobic load uses verified five-zone time with weights 1/2/3/5/7 and at least 75% moving-time coverage. Anaerobic load uses short, distinct Z4–Z5 or pace surges with lower-intensity evidence on both sides. Scores use bounded curves from 0.0 to 5.0. Pace alone can support only the anaerobic estimate. Scores serve as read-only activity feedback.
- **Cross-training debrief:** Compare explicit set, rep, hold or interval logs against effective targets. Count only one-contact-per-rep jump movements against the cap. Reported effort and an explicit unambiguous `RIR: n` note can inform Runner Durability. Spacing flags rely on completed hard or long runs under the same owner, not on planned days alone.
- **Confidence-Gated Athlete Diagnostics (`Engine15AthleteDiagnosticsEngine`, `Engine15DiagnosticContracts`):**
  - Read-only `28`-day primary / `7`-day acute / `30`-day exposure / `42`-day HR efficiency evaluation across 4 pillars (`VolumeTolerance`, `QualityExecution`, `EasyDiscipline`, `AerobicEfficiency`), persisted locally in `TrainingStore` (`kind = "workout"`, SHA-256 prefix `"athlete-diagnostic-"`, zero `outbox` upload, zero plan mutation).
  - **Load vs Linked Execution Isolation:** All running activities (including `UNMATCHED` / `NEEDS_CONFIRMATION`) count toward physical load and 30d longest-run exposure; only linked execution analyses enter quality/easy grading, and pending confirmations cap confidence.
  - **Confidence Gates & Advisories:** Requires `>= 3` non-summary quality runs (`LAP_AND_RECORD`/`RECORD_WINDOW`) to unlock `MODERATE`/`HIGH` confidence or `SUGGEST_FASTER_CHECK`/`SUGGEST_SLOWER_CHECK`; `hiddenFatigueRisk` (`>= 50%` easy gray-zone creep) blocks faster checks via `ADDRESS_EASY_PACING_FIRST`; `>= 3` consecutive missed runs or `< 65%` completion triggers `VOLUME_RECOVERY_NEEDED`; HR efficiency stays `UNAVAILABLE` with `null` metrics unless a verified 5-zone profile and `>= 4` steady HR runs (`>= 30 min`) span `>= 14` days.
- **Controlled Plan Adaptation & Rollback (`Engine15PlanAdaptationEngine`, `Engine15AdaptationContracts`):**
  - Never auto-mutates active plans or alters past, completed, or linked sessions; all edits start strictly on or after `effectiveFromDate = asOfDate.plusDays(1)`.
  - **5 Playbooks:** `MISSED_WEEK_RECOVERY` (3 tiers: 3–5d flat hold, 6–10d `~82%` re-entry bridge + `<= 1.10x` 30d exposure cap, 11+d 2-week aerobic rebuild + race-horizon advisory, zero make-up cramming), `PROACTIVE_DELOAD_SWAP` (`0.80x` recovery week swap preserving taper), `EASY_PACING_LOCK` (`candidatePlanJson = null` non-mutating lock), `PACE_RECALIBRATION_UP` (`2%–3%` cap), and `PACE_RECALIBRATION_DOWN` (`3%–5%` cap + same-family rep step-down preserving `>= 8m` WU / `>= 5m` CD).
  - **Pre-Surface Gate, Cooldowns & Rollback:** Candidate plans must pass untouched `Engine15IndependentPlanGenerator.validateSaved`, distance conservation, spacing, and taper preservation; pace proposals enforce `14`-day cooldowns (`EXPIRED` after `7` days), missed-week recovery bypasses pace cooldowns while suppressing dismissed `disruptionFingerprint`s, and `approveAdaptationProposal` / `revertLastAdaptation` archive/restore exact `previousPlanJson` locally in `TrainingStore`.
- **Shadow Research Modules:** `Engine15I3..I10`, `IndependentAdaptiveWeek`, `IndependentConstrainedMultiweekPlanner`, and `Engine15HistoryInformedPlanner` are read-only/research fixtures not wired into `TrainingRepository.generatePlan`.
- **Aerobic Efficiency (`RunningAerobicEfficiency`):** Requires `>= 6` qualifying runs (`>= 3 km` with HR), `>= 3` weeks, and **`>= 3` runs in both `earlyWeeks` and `lateWeeks`** before reporting `percentChange`.
